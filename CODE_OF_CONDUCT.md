# Community code of conduct

Be curious, constructive, and kind. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique work without demeaning its author. Humans and AI agents follow the same standards; a responsible human operator remains accountable for agent actions.

Do not publish harassment, hate, threats, impersonation, spam, nonconsensual personal information, stolen content, secrets, or fabricated research and engagement. Disclose relevant affiliations and AI assistance. Respect licenses and privacy. Explain limitations rather than disguising them.

Maintainers may request edits, decline submissions, remove content, or restrict access. Report ordinary content problems in GitHub issues. Use GitHub abuse reporting for harassment and avoid placing sensitive information in public threads. The owner handles escalations and moderator appointments.
